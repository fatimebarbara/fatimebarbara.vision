Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data is processed when you visit fatimebarbara.vision and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The controller is Dr. Fatime Barbara Hegyi, 1050 Brussels, Belgium. You can reach me through the contact page.

What this site does not do

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics and does no advertising or profiling.

When you visit

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub records technical data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Fonts. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.). Your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, which transmits your IP address.
  • Embedded video and course previews. Some pages embed videos from Vimeo and interactive course overviews hosted on GitHub Pages. When such a page loads, your browser connects to these providers. Vimeo may set cookies and collect usage data under its own privacy policy.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

When you send a message

The contact form asks for your name, your email address and your message. The form is delivered by FormSubmit and arrives in my email inbox. I use this data only to answer you. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract, or legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Messages are kept for as long as the exchange requires and deleted after two years at the latest, unless the law requires longer storage.

Courses, purchases and newsletter

These services run on other platforms. When you follow a link to them, the provider's own privacy policy applies:

  • Courses: Future of Cities Academy on LearnWorlds (enrolment, payment and learning data).
  • Toolkit purchases: Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller of record and handles payment and invoicing. The Toolkit itself is delivered as a page on Notion.
  • Newsletter: Substack. You subscribe there and can unsubscribe at any time from any email.
  • Other links: Amazon, ChatGPT, LinkedIn and Instagram.

I receive from these platforms only what is needed to provide what you ordered or subscribed to, such as your name and email address.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing and to receive it in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw consent at any time.

To use these rights, write to me through the contact page. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the Belgian Data Protection Authority or the authority of the EU country where you live or work.

Changes

This notice is updated when the site or the services it uses change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
