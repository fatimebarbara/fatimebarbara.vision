Research areas

Four bodies of work, one underlying question.

Across leadership, innovation, monitoring and adjacent fields, the work converges on one inquiry: how do places build the structural capacity to learn, adapt and improve over time?

Research area 01

Publications on leadership.

This research looks at what urban leadership does for a place: how it changes organisations, places and their ability to adapt.

Publications focus on the interconnectedness of leadership, governance systems, stakeholder engagement and monitoring and evaluation, and how these factors influence a city’s ability to become resilient in the face of social and environmental change.

View leadership publications

Research area 02

Publications on innovation ecosystems.

Research focused on understanding the dynamics of innovation ecosystems, from their formation and measurement to their impact on urban and regional competitiveness. Using data analysis and case studies, it looks at what makes innovation districts and place-based innovation succeed.

The publications also examine the role of emerging technologies in shaping these ecosystems, and how technology, policy and urban development interact.

View innovation publications

Research area 03

Publications on monitoring and evaluation.

Places can only improve what they can see. This research focuses on the development and evaluation of effective monitoring and evaluation systems for place-based research and innovation strategies.

By analysing real-world case studies, the work explores how these systems can support policy learning, inform future policy decisions and contribute to achieving sustainable development goals.

Research area 04

Other publications.

A broader collection of publications across diverse fields, from artificial intelligence and emerging technologies to identification of reference regions, university-industry connectivity, urban mobility and regional cooperation.

They follow the connections between innovation, technology and place that do not fit neatly into a single field.
