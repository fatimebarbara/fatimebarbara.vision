Research area 01 · Publications

Publications on leadership.

Research on the role of urban and regional leadership in driving positive change: how leadership shapes governance, stakeholder engagement and the capacity of places to adapt.

Book

The Future of Cities: A Data-Driven Approach to Tackling Grand Challenges and Building Sustainable, Resilient Communities

Looks at how mayors and other place leaders can use data and citizen science to tackle grand challenges such as sustainability, health, education, security and poverty. Using real-world examples and data analysis, it shows how data-driven leadership helps cities find solutions that work for local communities, drawing on the collective intelligence that citizen science brings.

View book page

Technical report

Joint Research Centre Technical Report on Factors of Leadership

This report on the “Factors of Leadership Attitude Enhancing Interregional Collaboration” explores how the leadership of cross-border partnerships affects motivation and commitment within the partnership, by comparing attitudes of leaders and participants to explore the potential for more effective operation. The assessment framework has been developed to assist leading stakeholders of interregional collaborative actions to ensure efficiency, sustainability and success of their projects.

Encyclopedia chapter

The Palgrave Encyclopedia of Urban and Regional Futures

The chapter on “Urban and Regional Leadership” presents the interdependence of leadership on governance systems, stakeholders’ involvement and monitoring and evaluation linked to research and innovation strategies, building on the notion of leadership contributing to transforming places to become resilient in adapting to social and environmental changes.

Technical report

Joint Research Centre Technical Report

The article on “Leadership Impact on Motivation and Commitment in Interregional Collaborations” pilots an assessment framework that evaluates the impact of leadership on motivation and commitment in cross-border collaborative actions. Regularly assessing this impact contributes to the efficiency and sustainability of collaborative actions by signalling issues of motivation and commitment.

Article

Leadership to Address Urban Environmental Challenges

Looks at the context and impact of leadership at the urban level in actions addressing specific agendas related to climate and the environment, providing an insight into how leadership contributes to transforming places, organisations and capabilities, leading to better adaptation to social, economic and environmental changes.
