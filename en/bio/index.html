About Fatime

Data, cities and the people in them.

Data matters when it helps people make better decisions about the places they live in.

Fatime Barbara Hegyi is an urban researcher, author and artist. Her work looks at cities as living systems: how they learn, where they get stuck and what helps them improve.

Her book, The Future of Cities: A Data-Driven Approach to Tackling Grand Challenges and Building Sustainable, Resilient Communities, looks at how mayors and other place leaders can use data and citizen science to tackle sustainability, health, education, security and poverty.

On stage, she turns complex urban research into something people can act on. She speaks to audiences in government, academia and business, from mayors working on social progress to companies aligning their strategy with sustainability. She has also been interviewed by CNN.

Her work follows the connections between health, education, mobility, technology and governance. One idea runs through all of it: political will can be treated as a renewable resource when it is guided by evidence and shared purpose.

Speech topics

Five talks.

Each talk comes out of the research and the book, and is adapted to the audience, whether it is a keynote stage or a closed-door policy roundtable.

01
Data · Analytics · Smart cities

Decoding the Urban DNA

How data is building smarter, more human-centered cities.

Cities are living organisms of information, producing data all the time. This talk looks at how that data can improve urban planning and management, and why so much of what cities collect never reaches a decision.

Adapts for: Policymakers seeking evidence-based decision frameworks · Corporate leaders exploring data-driven urban strategy · Smart-city summits · Public-sector innovation conferences

02
Business · Innovation · Strategy

Urban Innovation

Data-driven strategies for corporate growth and social impact.

Companies shape cities, often more than policy does, but usually without a clear map of what a city needs. This talk looks at how businesses can become real partners in building better cities.

Adapts for: Corporate offsites and leadership retreats · ESG and sustainability programmes · Boardroom strategy sessions · Cross-sector partnership convenings

03
Sustainability · Climate · Urban design

The Greenprint Revolution

Envisioning and building the sustainable city of tomorrow.

The climate crisis asks us to change how we design and manage our cities. The Greenprint Revolution is a framework for doing that in practice, bringing together data and community-led initiatives.

Adapts for: Policymakers shaping climate and environmental strategy · Corporate ESG programmes · Mayor convenings · Climate conferences

04
Health · Well-being · Urban planning

Cities and Health

How healthier people create healthier cities.

The future of health walks the streets we walk, savours the food we can reach, breathes the air of our green spaces. Much of it is decided by the city itself. This talk looks at the link between urban design and well-being.

Adapts for: Health-system policy bodies and public-health convenings · Corporate wellness and insurance summits · Hospital networks · Health-and-cities programmes

05
Education · Innovation · Lifelong learning

The Urban Learning Lab

Data-driven education for urban transformation.

Education shapes what a city can become. This talk looks at how urban spaces can work as “Learning Labs” for lifelong learning and innovation, using data to see where they are needed most.

Adapts for: University and academic leadership retreats · Education policymakers · EdTech conferences · Foundations and philanthropy convenings
